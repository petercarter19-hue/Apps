# The messages to send

1. [kickoff.txt](kickoff.txt): paste this first, after changing the [brackets] (see section 4 of [KIT.md](../KIT.md)). It builds the whole app step by step, and after each step you say "Works. Next step."
2. [kickoff-all-in-one-go.txt](kickoff-all-in-one-go.txt): the same message, told not to stop between steps.
3. [friend-version.txt](friend-version.txt): when you want a clean, offline installer to give to a friend, tested in Windows Sandbox.
