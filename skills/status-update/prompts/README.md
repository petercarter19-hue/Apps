# The messages

Everything you type to the AI to add the status-update skill and try it, in order. Open a file, copy all of it, and paste it into your AI app. The [brackets] are your choices; [KIT.md](../KIT.md), section 4, explains each one.

1. **[add-the-skill.txt](add-the-skill.txt):** the one message that adds the skill. It carries the whole skill, so the AI can save it exactly.
   - Or **[add-the-skill-from-a-download.txt](add-the-skill-from-a-download.txt)** if you downloaded this folder instead.
2. **Start a new chat** in the same folder, so the skill loads.
3. **Practice with a made-up project,** one message at a time. [KIT.md](../KIT.md), section 6, says what to look for in each reply and shows a real one:
   - **[try-1-practice-status.txt](try-1-practice-status.txt):** a made-up bake sale website, and "Give me a status update."
   - **[try-2-whats-waiting.txt](try-2-whats-waiting.txt):** a choice only you can make, and "What's waiting on me?"
   - **[try-3-one-event-note.txt](try-3-one-event-note.txt):** one thing finishes, so you get a short note.
4. **[try-4-your-own-project.txt](try-4-your-own-project.txt):** on your own work, just ask:

   ```text
   Give me a status update.
   ```

5. **Make it yours:** [KIT.md](../KIT.md), section 7, has messages for shorter updates, your time zone, a sixth part, and more.
6. **[share-with-a-friend.txt](share-with-a-friend.txt):** a zip of the skill folder to send a friend.
