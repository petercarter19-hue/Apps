App rules

When you dictate, the tool checks which app you're in. A style only steers punctuation and layout in dictation, never your words. "No tidy-up" pastes exactly what you said (terminals and code). List several program names with commas to share one rule.

Voice shortcuts

Say "open …" and the phrase. Only what's listed here (plus apps and files by name) can be opened, and nothing is ever run as a command. Programs and scripts are never opened from a file shortcut. A Windows shortcut (.lnk) opens only if you list it by its exact name.

Meetings

Records you and the call. When it ends you get a summary, decisions, action items and the transcript in a Word document in your Meeting notes folder. It always asks first, and it is off until you turn it on in Settings.

Not recording

Voice notes

"Note: …" goes to
Hold Left Windows and say "note: …" (or "remember that…"). Each note is saved as a time-stamped file. A folder works with Obsidian: point it at your vault.

Read it to me

Voice and speed
Select text anywhere, hold Left Windows and say "read this to me". Esc stops it. These are the voices built into Windows (free, and nothing leaves your PC). For more of them: Settings › Time & language › Speech › Manage voices.
